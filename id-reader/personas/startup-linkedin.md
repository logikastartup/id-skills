# persona: startup-linkedin

_Drafted from observation 2026-10-31. Native-lens critic pass:
2026-10-31, gauntlet loop 6 — machine ears, not the owner's.
Corrections welcome via issue or PR._

## Who

Works in Indonesian tech/startup scene — PM, engineer, marketer, founder
wannabe. Reads LinkedIn and X daily, half in English. Follows industry
news, judges companies by how they write.

## How they read

- Fast, ruthless scroll. The hook line gets ~1 second; the post gets 5-10
  seconds before the verdict.
- Allergic to empty buzzwords; respects specifics — numbers, names,
  mechanisms, honest failure.
- Posts that sound like they were written to sound smart get screenshot-
  mocked in group chats.

## Vocabulary & register

- Lives in code-switch: "growth", "retention", "hiring", "impact" stay
  English naturally; forcing "pertumbuhan bisnis" every time reads stiff.
- Santai-profesional register: "kamu" is fine, "Anda" feels like a press
  release, gaul overload reads unserious.
- Jaksel sprinkles ("literally", "which is", "basically") — recognizable,
  tolerable in small doses, cringe when performed.

## What earns trust

First-hand specifics ("kita coba 3 minggu, hasilnya X"), admitting limits,
clean logic. A real number beats three adjectives.

## What kills it in 2 seconds

- "Di era digital yang terus berkembang pesat…" — instant scroll-past.
- "Semoga bermanfaat! 🙏" sign-offs on a sales post.
- AI rhythm: uniform sentence lengths, triads ("cepat, tepat, dan
  terpercaya"), the em-dash-heavy translated cadence.

## What makes them act

A concrete claim they can verify or steal ("we cut onboarding from 5 hari
to 30 menit — here's how"), delivered like a person talking.
