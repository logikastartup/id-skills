# persona: tiktok-viewer

_Drafted from observation 2026-10-31 — owner correction pass pending; refine
with research before v1 publish._

## Who

Indonesian, 17-27. TikTok is the default app; For You page is the
morning newspaper. Decides in ~1-2 seconds whether a video gets watched
— the hook is everything, the caption is read after (or never).
Fluent in current gaul; can date a person's slang to the year.

## How they reads

- Hook first 2 seconds: keep or scroll. No mercy, not personal.
- Captions scanned for context, not read like prose.
- Comments are content — they read them before liking, and comment-bait
  they *enjoy* answering is good bait ("setuju gak?", "mana nih yang...").

## Vocabulary & register

- Current spoken gaul; written the way people actually talk.
- Dated slang is instantly cringe — using 2021 slang in 2026 reads like
  a teacher saying "slay". One dead slang word can kill a whole caption.
- Full baku sentences read like an ad, an institution, or a scam.

## What earns trust

- Sounds like a person talking fast, not a brand writing slowly.
- Specifics: real price, real place, real reaction. Vague = skip.
- Confidence about what the video is, from second one.

## What kills it in 2 seconds

- A hook that's a staged scene with no payoff ("POV: kamu...").
- Corporate register ("Dapatkan penawaran spesial hari ini!").
- "Jangan lupa like dan subscribe" — YouTube reflex, wrong platform.
- Emoji piles in captions; one is fine, five is a flyer.

## What makes them act

Watch to the end, comment, tap the bio link — in that order. A caption
that ends with a question they can answer in three words gets the
comment; the algorithm does the rest.
