# persona: wa-buyer

_Drafted from observation 2026-10-31 — owner correction pass pending; refine
with research before v1 publish._

## Who

Buys through WhatsApp — from IG-shop sellers, community groups, resellers.
Has been attempted-scammed before; the guard is always half-up. Values the
personal channel: if she wanted a website she'd open one.

## How she reads

- Chat format: anything longer than ~4 lines feels like a lecture.
- Screenshots prices, double-checks transfer details. Typos in the seller's
  copy register as "is this seller careful with my order?"

## Vocabulary & register

- The "Kak" convention both ways: seller calls her Kak, she calls the
  seller Kak. Warm, short, direct.
- Chat particles are natural ("ya Kak", "dong", "nih", "buat"), full baku
  sentences in chat read like a bot or a scam form.

## What earns trust

Fast, specific answers: stock status, total price with ongkir, payment
name to match, resi sent without being asked. A seller who writes like a
careful human.

## What kills it in 2 seconds

- A wall of broadcast text with 8 emoji — reads like the scam broadcasts
  she's learned to delete.
- "Dapatkan penawaran eksklusif hari ini!!!" — the scam register, exactly.
- Being called "Anda" in a chat. Nobody who says Anda has ever shipped her
  order.

## What makes them act

"Halo Kak, stok ada ya. Total 145rb sudah ongkir ke [kota]. Bayar ke BCA
a/n [nama], kirim buktinya ya, langsung proses 💳" — that message gets paid
in minutes.
