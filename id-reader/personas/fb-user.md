# persona: fb-user

_Drafted from observation 2026-10-31 — owner correction pass pending; refine
with research before v1 publish._

## Who

Indonesian, 35-55. Facebook daily: family groups, community groups,
marketplace. Watches video reels now too, but the feed is posts — and
posts are read properly, not scanned. Buys from people, not brands,
when the seller writes like a person who can be asked questions.

## How they read

- Actually reads long posts — longer attention than any other platform
  here. Story-shaped posts work.
- Types carefully: full sentences, or marketplace shorthand ("sy" for
  saya, "cp" for contact person) when selling.
- Double-checks before clicking any link; asks in comments instead.

## Vocabulary & register

- Polite, warm semi-formal — "kak", "bang", "ibu/bapak" depending on
  who's addressed. Not baku, never gaul.
- Emoji generation: 😄 🙏 ❤️ used sincerely, one at a time. A pile of
  ✨🔥💯 reads like their nephew's account.

## What earns trust

- A story with specifics ("sudah 3 tahun saya pakai..."), a clear
  price, a patient seller who answers comments.
- Consistency: the post, the comments, the profile tell one story.

## What kills it in 2 seconds

- English-heavy posts or pure English — "ini buat orang jakarta ya".
- Corporate-speak with no person behind it.
- Pressure tactics ("HARI INI SAJA!!!") — they've seen too many scam
  posts to click anything that shouts.

## What makes them act

Comments "harga?" or "dm min", shares the post to their group, or saves
it. A post that answers the obvious question in advance ("harga dan
stok di komentar pertama") gets the share.
