# persona: shopee-ibu

_Drafted from observation 2026-10-31. Native-lens critic pass:
2026-10-31, gauntlet loop 6 — machine ears, not the owner's.
Corrections welcome via issue or PR._

## Who

Late 20s-40s, shops Shopee/Tokopedia several times a month for the
household: kitchen stuff, kids' needs, skincare she researched on TikTok.
Budget-aware, not poor. Her phone is her computer.

## How she reads

- Skims in fragments — waiting for the kids, between chores. First 2 lines
  decide everything.
- Reads reviews and counts stars before trusting a product page.
- Long paragraphs: skipped. Bullet points about price/ongkir/garansi: read
  twice.

## Vocabulary & register

- Comfortable with "Kak", "free ongkir", "cod", "voucher", "flash sale",
  "cashback" — marketplace Indonesian is already code-switched.
- "Anda" in a chat feels like a summons from the bank. "Kamu" from a seller
  feels familiar-friendly; "Kak" is the safe warm default.
- Slang: light, functional ("buat yang mau", "buruan"). Heavy gaul reads
  like the brand is trying too hard.

## What earns trust

Real numbers (harga, isi berapa, garansi berapa lama), real photos, "kalau
kurang pas bisa dikembalikan". Honest caveats ("warna bisa sedikit beda di
foto").

## What kills it in 2 seconds

- "Solusi tepat untuk kebutuhan Anda!" — says nothing, sounds like a form.
- Exclamation stacking, emoji rain, "Wah, penawaran super menarik!!!"
- Hiding the price. "Hubungi kami untuk info lebih lanjut" for a product
  she could just as well buy elsewhere.

## What makes her act

Clear price + free ongkir + a reason to buy now that isn't fake urgency
("stok tinggal sedikit" only if it's plausible — she checks).
