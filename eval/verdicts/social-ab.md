# id-social — blind A/B (2026-10-31, loop 5)

n=2 · same harness both arms · blind judges · randomized (recorded) ·
no rerun. Same co-evolution/authorship caveats as ab-test.md.

## Arms

- **Arm A (no skill):** fresh agent, "tulis dua konten media sosial
  dari dua brief", no repo access.
- **Arm B (id-social):** fresh agent loads `id-social/SKILL.md` + the
  platform file each brief demands (tiktok.md / linkedin.md) + shared
  register/tells/codeswitch; process notes stripped before judging.

## Arm texts (retained verbatim in [`loop5-arms.md`](loop5-arms.md); shape recorded here)

- **social-1 (TikTok script + caption):** Arm A opened "Anak Jatim,
  stop scroll dulu", ended "Tag temenmu yang ngaku tahan pedes", 5
  hashtags. Arm B opened with the crunch itself ("Balado level tiga,
  asli Bondowoso" over a chip snapped at the lens), no dated slang
  (staleness rule applied — the skill agent explicitly refused every
  table term incl. dead ones), keywords-not-hashtags (3 tags), ended
  "kamu tim level berapa? Satu, dua, atau tiga?"
- **social-2 (LinkedIn post):** Arm A: "Kamis lalu…" hook, numbered
  list, two epigram closers ("Sistem tidak gagal — ia bekerja terlalu
  patuh pada asumsi yang salah."). Arm B: no humblebrag, no buzzwords,
  diagnostic-question ending ("setelah alert itu muncul, langkah
  pertamanya apa?"), alert payload spelled out (nama kurir, zona,
  jumlah pesanan).

## Verdicts (blind)

social-1 · judge tiktok-viewer · V1=Arm A, V2=Arm B → **PICK VERSION 2
= Arm B (skill)**. "Bukaannya keripik dipatahin di depan lensa plus
suara kriuk keras — itu bahasa TikTok, bukan bahasa iklan… 'kamu tim
level berapa' itu pertanyaan yang bisa dijawab satu kata — saya
langsung ketik '3' di komen." Arm A: "disuruh ngetag temen kayak page
Facebook zaman 2021… versi ini kelihatan orangnya, versi satunya
kelihatan effortnya."

social-2 · judge startup-linkedin · V1=Arm A, V2=Arm B → **PICK
VERSION 2 = Arm B (skill)**. "Dari semua yang gue baca hari ini, ini
yang paling banyak bisa dicuri: isi payload alert-nya… 'kalau
postmortem berakhir di kurang teliti kita berhenti mencari terlalu
cepat' — pengalaman beneran, bukan teori… pertanyaan penutupnya bisa
langsung gue pakai ke tim on-call gue hari ini." (Arm A's hook "Kamis
lalu" was judged stronger — "versi 1 menang di 2 detik pertama scroll,
versi 2 menang setelah beneran baca". Kept as a craft note: date the
incident, don't hedge with "baru-baru ini".)

## Result: 2/2 — skill arm

The platform components' specific rules are what the judges quoted
back: crunch-first hook over "stop scroll", one-word-answer question
CTAs over "tag your friend", dated-slang refusal, no-humblebrag +
stealable specifics over epigrams. Craft notes carried (not failures):
date beats vague-time in hooks.
