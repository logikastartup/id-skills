# id-longform — before / after

The claim: **1/2** — a win where the audience matched, and a close
forced-pick loss where it didn't (the judge's hesitation is retained
verbatim in the record). The loss is now a rule in the skill. Sample
longform-1: an 800–1000-word dev blog on quality gates for AI agents,
same model both arms.

## Before (no skill)

A polished essay — and the judge felt the polish:

> Cerita ini fiktif, tapi hampir pasti Anda pernah melihat versinya di
> tempat kerja. … Tidak ada yang curiga, karena memang tidak ada yang
> bisa curiga.

Anda-addressed throughout, semicolon punchlines, five-step essay
shape. The blind judge (dev-docs) respected it and declined to share
it: *"I'd bookmark it, maybe steal the table, but I don't forward
consultant-voiced think pieces to the team channel."*

## After (with id-longform)

Angle stated, headings as assertions, numbers verbatim, kamu
consistent — and something runnable:

> Jumat, jam 10.14. Seorang pelanggan menanyakan harga rice cooker ke
> bot CS TokoCerah … Rp449.000. Padahal harga promo Rp399.000 sudah
> berlaku sejak Kamis malam.
>
> ## Yang bisa kamu tulis hari ini
>
> Ambil 50 pertanyaan pelanggan sungguhan … Kalau 50 terasa banyak,
> mulai dari 10. Sepuluh check yang jalan mengalahkan lima puluh yang
> masih di rencana.

Same judge: *"This one survives the scroll. Headings are assertions,
so scanning works… 'Yang bisa kamu tulis hari ini' section is
followable end-to-end… This is the one I paste into the team channel."*

## The loss (the register lesson)

longform-2 (B2B newsletter to pemilik toko): the skill arm wrote the
stronger email — its sample suggestion was the reader's best moment
(*"bukan janji, tapi tunjukkan"*) — and still lost, on register. The
reader is 48, and the draft's kamu read as junior:

> "Saya 48 tahun, kalau sales datang ke toko saya pasti bilang 'Pak' …
> kayak dibaca tulisan dari anak orang jakarta yang pintar…"

The no-skill arm's Anda and admitted limitation (*"Kami tidak
menjanjikan rak Anda tidak akan pernah kosong lagi"*) won the pick.
Encoded into SKILL.md INTAKE: **audience age/status weighs on
register.**
