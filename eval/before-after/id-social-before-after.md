# id-social — before / after

The claim: **4/5 across two rounds** (loop 5: 2/2; loop 7: 2/3 — the
loss taught the CTA-reason rule, encoded into tiktok.md same-day) —
and in both rounds the platform components' specific rules are what
the judges quoted back. Sample social-1: a 30-second TikTok script +
caption for a Bondowoso cassava-chip brand entering Indomaret East
Java, same model both arms, brief in the sample file.

## Before (no skill)

Ad-shaped from frame one:

> [0-3 detik] … VO: "Anak Jatim, stop scroll dulu. Ada yang baru di
> Indomaret." … VO: "Gas ke Indomaret terdekat. Tag temenmu yang ngaku
> tahan pedes, uji dia di level 3."

Five hashtags, tag-a-friend CTA. The blind judge (tiktok-viewer):
*"dari detik pertama udah kerasa ini iklan yang mau viral… ditutup
suruh ngetag temen kayak page Facebook zaman 2021."*

## After (with id-social)

The crunch is the hook — and the staleness rule refused every dated
slang term in the table (the dead ones included):

> [0:00-0:02 · HOOK] Visual: tangan merobek kemasan, keripik
> dipatahkan tepat di depan lensa, suara kriuk keras. VO: "Balado
> level tiga, asli Bondowoso."
>
> [0:23-0:30 · CTA] … VO: "Jadi sekarang, jujur: kamu tim level
> berapa? Satu, dua, atau tiga?"

Three keyword hashtags, not five. Same judge: *"Bukaannya keripik
dipatahin di depan lensa plus suara kriuk keras — itu bahasa TikTok,
bukan bahasa iklan… 'kamu tim level berapa' itu pertanyaan yang bisa
dijawab satu kata — saya langsung ketik '3' di komen, nggak perlu
mikir."* And the line
that summarizes the whole A/B: *"versi ini kelihatan orangnya, versi
satunya kelihatan effortnya…"*

## The second sample

social-2 (LinkedIn incident post): the skill arm dropped the
humblebrag and the epigram closers and spelled out the alert payload
(nama kurir, zona, jumlah pesanan). Judge (startup-linkedin): *"Dari
semua yang gue baca hari ini, ini yang paling banyak bisa dicuri…"*
and of the closing question, *"bisa langsung gue pakai ke tim on-call
gue hari ini…"* (The no-skill arm's "Kamis lalu" hook was judged
stronger — kept as a craft note: date the incident, don't hedge.)

**Loop 7 (three new briefs — TikTok thrift, LinkedIn postmortem, FB
catering): 2/3.** The LinkedIn judge caught the no-skill arm quoting a
technically reversed index story (*"post engineering yang cerita
akarnya kebalik arah nggak bisa saya angkat"*) — the persona doing
real review work. The TikTok loss taught one rule, encoded same-day:
a question CTA must state WHAT ANSWERING GETS the viewer ("aku balas
satu-satu… nanti aku kasih tau masih ada atau nggak"), not just be
cheap to answer.
