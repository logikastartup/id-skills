# id-copy — before / after

The claim: same model, with the skill vs without, blind persona
readers — **5/5 skill-arm picks across two rounds** (loop 3: 2/2;
loop 7: 3/3 on WA promo, IG caption, marketplace listing). Quotes are
verbatim (elided with …, never reworded). Below is sample gen-1 in
full voice.

## Before (no skill)

The same model, plain instruction, no repo access — the scam-broadcast
register the persona deletes on sight:

> Halo Kak! 👜✨ Ada kabar baik buat hari ini: tas wanita premium
> koleksi terbaru lagi diskon spesial! … Stoknya nggak banyak, jadi
> siapa cepat dia dapat ya, Kak! … Selamat berbelanja, Kak!

Blind reader (wa-buyer) on exactly this text: *"the broadcast I
delete… Nobody who ends a message like that has ever sent me a resi.
Mute."*

## After (with id-copy)

Register stated up front (chat-santai, level 3), draft written against
the tells blacklist, a question instead of a fake close:

> Halo Kak 😊 Lagi ada promo tas hari ini... kualitas premium tapi
> harganya bikin kaget (yang enak). Stoknya beneran dikit, makanya
> jangan lama-lama! … Mau langsung order atau lihat katalog dulu?
> Balas chat ini ya Kak, nanti aku bantu pilih-in 👜

Same reader: *"reads like an actual seller… Mau langsung order atau
lihat katalog dulu? is a question I can answer in three words…"*

## What changed

- Hype opener + worn urgency ("siapa cepat dia dapat") → one concrete
  offer with an honest hedge ("harganya bikin kaget (yang enak)").
- Fake close ("Selamat berbelanja, Kak!") → a question the buyer can
  answer in three words — replies are the currency of WA commerce.
- The skill ships honesty notes with every draft ("verifikasi apakah
  diskon pembelian kedua benar-benar berlaku") — stripped before
  judging, retained in the record.
