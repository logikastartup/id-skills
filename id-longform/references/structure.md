# structure.md — long-form structure + scaffolding ban-list (id-longform)

`component: id-longform` · Research-backed (sources at bottom); owner
pass pending. Honesty rule throughout: **phrases in the ban-list are
weak signals, not proof** — humans use them too (Batam Pos warns the
same). They fail in *density*, and in long-form they fail hardest at
the opening and the close, where readers judge whether a person wrote
this (documented: readers detect AI by "cara sebuah tulisan membuka
dan menutup pembahasan").

## 1. Angle + hook

- The opener must earn the second paragraph with **specific
  information**, not scene-setting: the documented AI failure is
  openers that "tidak memberikan informasi spesifik" (Batam Pos on
  "Dalam lanskap yang terus berkembang"-class openers).
- Lived hook, not staged scene (tells #1 rule). A concrete moment, a
  number, a claim you'll defend — anything a reader can hold.
- Newsletter convention with receipts (Biba Bercerita, ~48k subs):
  **aphoristic deck-line** or direct-address **question title** —
  "Mengapa kita menghabiskan masa kecil untuk 'menjadi dewasa', lalu
  membayar psikolog untuk belajar menjadi anak lagi." First-person
  aku/kamu register is the genre norm, not a flaw.

## 2. Section moves

- Each section advances the angle; a section that only "introduces"
  is deleted. Subheads carry content ("Kenapa bot kamu menjawab
  'siap'"), never labels ("Pembahasan", "Latar Belakang").
- Paragraph rhythm varies: after any two same-length paragraphs,
  break the third. Documented AI tell: "Hampir setiap kalimat terasa
  tersusun dengan pola yang mirip" — uniform subject-predicate-object
  chains (Erafone) and "struktur kalimat sempurna… sampai membosankan"
  (Mahendradita, Medium).
- **The doctrine tension (encode, don't resolve):** kalimat efektif
  teaching (S-P-O-K order, baku diction) is good line-editing — and
  uniformly applied it produces exactly the uniform rhythm that reads
  as machine. Line-edit each sentence; then re-read the paragraph for
  rhythm and let a sentence start with the verb, drop its subject, or
  run short on purpose.

## 3. Scaffolding ban-list (density-weighted)

Documented ChatGPT-Indonesian crutches — zero tolerance at the opener
and closer, sparse tolerance in the body:

| Phrase family | Source |
|---|---|
| "Dalam lanskap yang terus berkembang…" openers | Batam Pos (ChatGPT-typical list) |
| "Sangat penting untuk dicatat bahwa…" | Batam Pos — flags emphasis the content doesn't earn |
| "di era digital yang serba cepat", "tak dapat dipungkiri" | BuildWithAngga ban-list |
| "pada akhirnya", "selain itu", "lebih lanjut" as connective crutches | BuildWithAngga |
| comma-stacking; semicolons + passive; no emotion marks ever | Mahendradita (Medium) |

Related but weaker (family-relevant, not yet documented as AI tells —
treat as craft rules, not tells): "para + noun" plural overkill
(doctrine: "para hadirin" is boros kata, id.wikipedia/Boros_kata);
"Kesimpulannya" as a mechanical closer (undocumented as a *tell*; the
documented rule is only that readers judge closes — so make the close
land a point; if the paragraph is a summary, cut it).

## 4. The close

- Lands the angle (a consequence, a next step, a cost of inaction) —
  or ends on the strongest concrete line available. It does not
  restate the outline.
- CTA conventions by type: newsletter = plain (the genre's norm is
  the platform-default Subscribe — Biba Bercerita; no shouting);
  blog = one clear next action, at most.

## 5. Facts + sourcing

- Claims carry specifics (numbers, names, dates) or are cut —
  over-explaining the general is a documented AI marker (Erafone).
- Credible-source norm even in casual registers (MenulisID editorial
  guide: "Gunakan sumber kredibel…"); community publications review
  before publishing — so does this skill's tells pass.
