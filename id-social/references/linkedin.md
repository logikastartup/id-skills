## Register

- **Indonesian-English mixing is the platform's default register**,
  read as "modern, professional, classy" in Indonesian workplaces
  (documented, Dec 2024) — canonical example of the genre's sound:
  *"Event-nya keren banget, vibes-nya dapet, dan host-nya really
  cool!"*. Codeswitch only where a term is standard tech/business
  vocabulary (per codeswitch.md); mixing as decoration is the tell.
- Semi-formal (ladder levels 2–3): not baku, never gaul. First
  person; reflection tone.

## Genre tells (the slop family this platform is famous for)

- **Humblebrag openers** — "Sering ditanya…", "Proud to share…",
  "Banyak yang DM saya soal…": the flexing discourse is LIVE inside
  Indonesian LinkedIn itself (critics: "pamer pencapaian, pamer
  narsum di sana sini"; defenders: with 7M+ job seekers, showcasing
  is survival). Write the lesson, not the trophy.
- **Motivation-post arcs** — orphan story → epiphany → "semoga
  bermanfaat". If the insight can't stand without the arc, it can't
  stand.
- **Buzzword stuffing** — Indonesia's most-used LinkedIn profile
  buzzwords were documented as "specialized" and "leadership" (2016
  data, historical): the local variant of global LinkedIn-ism.
- **Hashtag waves as genre events** — e.g. the #Desperate wave among
  Gen-Z job seekers (Oct 2024): real, and reading as despair-signaling
  to the same recruiters it targets. Don't ride wave tags.

## Craft (pattern-level — the documented gap above applies)

- **One idea in the first two lines** — feeds truncate; the hook is
  the thesis, not the setup.
- Short paragraphs, one screen beats one scroll.
- Concrete incident > announcement: what happened, what it cost, what
  changed. Numbers land on this platform.
- End with a question or an offered lesson — not "semoga bermanfaat".
