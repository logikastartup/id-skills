# facebook.md — Facebook Indonesia norms (id-social)

`component: id-social` · Research-backed (sources at bottom);
native-lens critic pass 2026-10-31 (gauntlet loop 6). Facebook is the
platform where demographic clichés die
hardest — the data below is the defensible version.

## Who is actually there (cite sources when quoting numbers)

- **Scale:** 122M users early 2025 / 121M late 2025 (DataReportal,
  Meta ad-reach methodology). NapoleonCat separately estimates ~203M
  (Sep 2026) — **methodologies disagree; never quote a number without
  naming its source.**
- **NOT an "old people's platform" in absolute terms:** the largest
  ad-audience band is 25–34 (37.8%), then 18–24 (28.3%) (NapoleonCat).
  The defensible claim is RELATIVE skew: Facebook is absent from Gen
  Z's top platforms (Jakpat Dec 2024: IG 81% · TikTok 70% · YouTube
  69%), and the 35–54 share (~23% combined) is the segment most other
  platforms lack. Male-skewing ad audience (57.9%) — opposite of IG.
- **Role in the stack (consensus, medium confidence):** community +
  conversion (Groups, Marketplace) where TikTok does awareness and IG
  does branding. **Don't crosspost identical content** — adapt per
  platform.

## Register + format

- **Informal, gaul-rich is DOCUMENTED** for Indonesian FB (journal
  study 2023: OTW, baper, gaje) — but calibrate to the cohort you're
  addressing: 35+ community posts read best in warm semi-formal
  (kak/bang/ibu-bapak, sincere single emoji 😄🙏❤️ — ladder levels
  2–3), younger groups run gaul (levels 3–4).
- **Long posts are read properly here** — story-shaped narrative
  works; this is the one platform in this skill where length is
  patience, not punishment.
- **~125 characters show before "See more"** (EN-source, medium
  confidence) — front-load the hook line regardless.
- **Marketplace/selling style** (observed consensus, URLs
  unverified): long narrative captions, "kak/ka" address, all-caps
  for genuine emphasis (not whole paragraphs).

## Platform tells

- English-heavy posts aimed at the 35+ Indonesian cohort ("ini buat
  orang Jakarta ya").
- Corporate-shout register ("HARI INI SAJA!!!") — this audience has
  the longest scam memory on the internet.
- Link-first posts with no person behind them; comments are where
  this platform converts — answer them.
